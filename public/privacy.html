Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-27

1. Overview

Encontrar Bicicleta Eletrica ("we", "us", "the app") is a mapping application that helps you locate shared bikes and electric-bike availability when operators provide that data. This privacy policy explains what data we access and how that data is handled.

2. Data We Access

  • Device location — With your explicit permission, we access device location to centre the map and search for nearby services. This data is not stored on our servers.
  • Map coordinates — When you pan/zoom the map, we send centre coordinates to third-party services for weather, air quality, EV stations and POIs.
  • Favourites — Stored exclusively on your device (localStorage). Never transmitted.

3. Third-Party Services

The app sends internet requests to external providers. Data transmitted may include your device location or map-centre coordinates:

OpenStreetMap / Leaflet

Map tiles for the interactive map. Tile servers may log IP and coordinates. © OSM contributors.

CityBikes API (api.citybik.es)

Bike network and station data. No user location is transmitted with this request.

Open-Meteo

Weather and air quality forecasts. Map-centre coordinates are sent whenever the map is panned and Smart Data is enabled.

OSRM (router.project-osrm.org)

Cycling routing from your location to a station. Exclusively user-triggered via Get Directions action.

Open Charge Map (api.openchargemap.io)

EV charging station data. Map-centre coordinates are sent when EV layer is enabled.

Overpass API (overpass-api.de)

Nearby toilets, water fountains, bike parking from OSM. Map-centre coordinates are sent when Amenities layer is enabled.

USGS

Recent global earthquake data. No location coordinates are sent with this request.

These providers may log requests subject to their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET — to load map graphics and exchange network data.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — centre the map on your position and provide area-specific services.

You can deny location permissions — the app will still work, defaulting to a global overview and manual city searches.

5. Data Storage & Retention

Location is session-memory only. Third-party providers may log requests. Favourites, language, and theme preferences are retained on your device and cleared when app data is cleared.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your Rights

You can revoke location permission in device settings at any time. Clearing app data removes all locally stored favourites and preferences.
